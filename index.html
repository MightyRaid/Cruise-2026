Penang Shore Plan (Choose Group Preference)
George Town Trishaw Tour + Air-Con Heritage Dinner (Best for 3yo, 5yo & Mum):
• 3:15 PM: Walk straight off Swettenham Pier (right in George Town) and hire 3–4 trishaws (~RM 40–50/trishaw/hr) to ride past Armenian Street murals, Clan Jetties, and Fort Cornwallis without tiring out Mum or the kids.
• 5:00 PM Early Dinner: Dine in air-conditioned comfort at Tek Sen Restaurant (Carnarvon St — famous double-roasted pork & home-style Chinese dishes) or Kebaya / ChinaHouse, then grab Grab cars back to the pier by 7:00 PM.
Minimal WalkingKids Love Trishaws
Penang Hill Funicular Railway + David Brown's / Cliff Cafe:
• 3:15 PM: Book two 6-seater Grab cars from Swettenham Pier to Penang Hill Lower Station (~25 mins). Take the fast funicular train up to cool 22°C hilltop views.
• 5:00 PM Early Dinner: Eat at the hilltop food court or David Brown's Restaurant before taking the train down by 6:45 PM (note: Friday evening funicular queues can take 30–45 mins).
